How it works

A clear, simple process — and you stay in control at every step.

  1. Submit your request

    Fill in the short request form with your business name, your Google Maps link, the service you need, and a description of what should change. You can attach photos if relevant.

  2. We review and confirm

    We look at your listing and your request, then email you to confirm the scope and the final price. Nothing starts until you agree.

  3. We prepare the update

    We prepare the change request carefully. Where Google requires the owner to approve or submit something in their own account, we guide you step by step — without ever needing your password.

  4. Google reviews the change

    Google decides whether to approve and publish the update, and how long that takes. Some changes appear quickly; others take longer or need more information. We keep you informed and tell you honestly if a change is declined.

What we need from you

  • The Google Maps link to your business listing
  • A clear description of what is wrong and what it should say
  • Photos or documents that support the change, if relevant
  • An email address where we can reach you

Tip: open your business in Google Maps, tap Share, then copy the link.

What we never ask for

  • Your Google account password
  • 2-step verification or security codes
  • Credit card numbers by email or in the request form

If anyone asks for these in our name, do not respond and contact us.

Common questions

How long will it take?

We aim to review requests promptly, but we don’t promise a specific turnaround. After a change is submitted, how long it takes to appear is up to Google.

What if Google declines the change?

It can happen — for example if more evidence is needed. We will tell you, explain what we know, and discuss options. See our Terms for how we handle refunds.

Can you update a listing I do not own?

No. You must be the owner or be authorized to manage the listing. We may ask you to confirm this.

How do I pay?

This website does not collect payments. We confirm the final price with you first, and share payment details only once you approve it.

Ready to fix your listing?

Tell us what needs changing. We review every request and confirm the scope and final price with you by email before any work begins.
