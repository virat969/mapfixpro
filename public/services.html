Our services

Five focused services to keep your Google Maps and Google Business Profile listing accurate.

Business name & address corrections

Fix a misspelled business name or an outdated or incorrect street address on your listing.

What’s included

  • Correct spelling or formatting of your business name
  • Update a street address, suite or unit number
  • Prepare the details Google typically asks for to review the change

Good for: Businesses that moved, rebranded, or noticed a typo or wrong suite number.

Request this service

Business opening hours updates

Make sure customers see the right hours, including holidays and temporary changes.

What’s included

  • Regular weekly opening hours
  • Holiday and special hours
  • Seasonal changes or temporary closures

Good for: Businesses whose hours changed, or that want accurate holiday hours.

Request this service

Business photo upload assistance

Get help preparing and submitting storefront, interior, product or team photos.

What’s included

  • Guidance on photo size, format and quality
  • Help choosing a logo and cover image
  • Help submitting photos for your listing

Good for: Businesses with missing, outdated or low-quality photos.

Location & map pin corrections

Move a misplaced map pin so customers are directed to your real entrance.

What’s included

  • Report a pin that sits in the wrong spot
  • Help describe the correct location clearly
  • Guidance for shared buildings, plazas and campuses

Good for: Businesses whose pin points to the wrong building, street or entrance.

Business information updates

Keep your phone number, website, category, description and other details accurate.

What’s included

  • Update phone number and website link
  • Review business category and description
  • Update services, attributes and other listing details

Good for: Businesses with old contact details or an incomplete listing.

Request this service

Good to know

  • You must be the owner of the business or authorized to manage its listing.
  • Google reviews and decides on every listing change.
  • We never ask for your Google password.
  • We confirm the final price with you before starting.

Not included

  • Guaranteed approval or guaranteed timing from Google.
  • Removing legitimate customer reviews or changing ratings.
  • Creating listings for businesses that do not exist.
  • Guaranteed search ranking or marketing results.
