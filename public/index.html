Quick answers

Do you need my Google password?

No. Never share your password or verification codes with anyone, including us. If an update needs access to your listing, you grant it yourself through Google’s official sharing or invitation tools.

Are you part of Google?

No. MapFixPro is an independent service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google.

Will Google definitely approve my change?

We can’t promise that. Google reviews every change and decides whether to publish it, and how long that takes. We prepare your request as clearly as possible and keep you updated.

Do I have to own the business?

You must be the owner or be authorized to manage the business listing. You confirm this when you submit a request.

Ready to fix your listing?

Tell us what needs changing. We review every request and confirm the scope and final price with you by email before any work begins.
