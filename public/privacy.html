Privacy Policy

How we handle your information.

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Template notice for the site owner: this policy is a starting template, not legal advice. Review it with a qualified attorney and adjust it to match your real data practices before launch.

This Privacy Policy explains what information MapFixPro (“MapFixPro”, “we”, “us”) collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this website you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us:

  • Service request details: your name, business name, email address, Google Maps link, requested service, request option, description of changes, and any photos you upload.
  • Contact messages: your name, email address, topic and message.
  • Basic technical data: like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and reliability. When you submit a form we also use a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address to limit repeated or automated submissions; we do not store your raw IP address with your request.

2. What we do not collect

  • We never ask for your Google password, verification codes or security answers. Do not send them to us.
  • This website does not collect payment card details.
  • This website does not use advertising trackers.

3. How we use your information

  • To review your request, confirm scope and pricing, and contact you about it.
  • To prepare and guide listing update requests you asked us to handle.
  • To answer your messages and provide support.
  • To keep records, prevent abuse and fraud, and meet legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us run the service, who may process data on our behalf: Netlify (website hosting and private storage of submitted requests and photos), Google Gmail (the email through which we receive a copy of each request, including attached photos), and, if enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile (a spam check on the forms).
  • Payment processors, if and when we offer online payment, only as needed to process payment.
  • Google, to the extent your request involves submitting information about your business listing. Information you ask us to put on a public listing may become publicly visible.
  • Authorities or advisers, when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

5. Photos you upload

Photos are used only to prepare and support your request. They are stored privately with our hosting provider (they are not publicly accessible) and a copy is emailed to us with your request. Large photos are resized in your browser before they are sent. Do not upload images that contain sensitive personal information or that you do not have the right to share.

6. How long we keep information

We keep request and message data for as long as needed to provide the service, keep business records and meet legal or accounting obligations. After that we delete or anonymize it.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, but no website or storage method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Depending on where you live, including California, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity first.

9. Children

This website is for business owners and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Third-party links

This website may link to third-party sites, including Google. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest version.
